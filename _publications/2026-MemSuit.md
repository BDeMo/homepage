Zhenyu Lei, **Mingjia Shi**, Xingbo Fu, Haoyu He, Qi R. Wang, Jundong Li.

Accepted at **EMNLP 2026**.

MemSuit grounds conversational memory summarization in downstream utility. A teacher with access to query-answer pairs generates self-contained, utility-aware memory entries; a student learns to produce them from raw conversations alone. Contrastive retriever tuning aligns retrieval with these compact entries to improve downstream question answering.
