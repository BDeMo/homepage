**Mingjia Shi**, Yinhan He, Yaochen Zhu, Jundong Li.

Accepted at **NeurIPS 2026**. Mingjia Shi is the first author.

Saliency-Aware Principle Selection (SAP) is a training-free inference-time method for vision-language agents. It evolves high-level reasoning principles, explores short parallel reasoning routes, and uses visual grounding tools as advisory evidence to reduce object hallucination under a comparable token budget.

The linked [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16702) is the earlier version, titled *Saliency-Aware Multi-Route Thinking: Revisiting Vision-Language Reasoning*.
